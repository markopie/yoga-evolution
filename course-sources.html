Yoga Evolution

Book sources and course lessons

Teacher recommendation means the authors give a practice instruction involving the lessons shown. Observed earlier practice means the selected pose or variation appears in an earlier part of the same book. The lesson lists below show the catalogue numbers used to identify each practice.

  • Light on Yoga
  • Yoga: A Gem for Women

  • Light on Pranayama

Light on Yoga

B. K. S. Iyengar, Light on Yoga, Appendix I, asana courses.

The three courses organise practice in blocks of weeks. To find an earlier occurrence, compare the pose entries in two blocks. An “Important Asanas” list has no week of introduction. A milestone follows its assigned week and review weeks revisit earlier work.

Yoga: A Gem for Women

Geeta S. Iyengar, Yoga: A Gem for Women, introductory programme and the elementary, intermediate and advanced yearly programmes.

The introductory practice comes before the three yearly phases. Within a year, the day sheets rotate and may be used many times. An earlier-practice suggestion looks back to a preceding phase. An early or developing version of a sheet belongs to the same authored lesson.

Yoga: The Iyengar Way

Silva, Mira and Shyam Mehta, Yoga: The Iyengar Way (1990), appendix, “Suggested Scheme of Practice.”

Course I · alternating lessons

The authors pair Lessons 1 and 2 for the first week, then Lessons 2 and 3 for the next. The pair moves forward one lesson at a time. Their advice is to practise the two whole lessons during that week. The search separately identifies where the focus pose occurs.

Course II · month three

In the third month the authors alternate groups 2 and 3 and revisit group 1 within each lettered practice group. For example, they pair 2B and 3B and include 1B in that month’s sitting practice. The first month already uses groups 1 and 2 together. See page 177.

Course III · later review

After completing group 4 and repeating the lessons, the authors bring adjacent numbered groups together for review across A–D. Lessons 2B and 3B belong to the same later review cycle. On the first pass, the scheme calls for alternating with Course II and leaves the choice of lesson open. See page 181.

Course IV offers six different daily emphases. These are parallel choices for practice.

How to Use Yoga

Mira Mehta, How to Use Yoga, ten-week practice course.

Some day sheets recur several times within their week. Earlier-practice suggestions use a preceding week and compare recovery sheets with other recovery sheets. Week 5 revisits Weeks 3 and 4. In Week 10 the practitioner chooses from Weeks 6–9.

Light on Pranayama

B. K. S. Iyengar, Light on Pranayama, five practice courses from Preparatory through Highly Intense.

Read a numbered block within its course and check the stage of the breathing practice. Review periods reuse earlier material. The intermediate consolidation cycle and the final weekly practice contain repeating days. An earlier occurrence of a stage gives you a place to revisit that stage.
